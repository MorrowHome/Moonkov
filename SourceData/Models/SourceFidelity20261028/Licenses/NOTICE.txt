SourceMaterialNodes.hlsl is an HLSL adaptation of the source-compatible
Perlin gradients, dimensional normalization, fBm and Jenkins hashes from
Blender Cycles. Changes: HLSL types and intrinsics, loop-based corner
summation, graph compiler integration and material-context interfaces.
